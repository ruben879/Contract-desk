**Staples vs situational (Ruben, Nov 3, 2026):** the standard offer packet is built from the staples — 20-19 (always), 40-11 (when financed), 56-0 (pre-1978), 49-1 (appraisal terms). The 36-11 HOA addendum is NOT a staple: it joins the packet only when the property is subject to mandatory POA membership (the MLS pull suggests it when the listing shows one).

## Note for the builder: the 20-19 ships in two editions

TREC's public-download 20-19 is FLAT (verified: no /AcroForm, zero widget annotations; kept in this folder as TREC-20-19-OfficialFlat-Reference.pdf). The fillable edition, distributed through TREC's license-holder channel, was supplied by Ruben Trujillo on Nov 3, 2026 and is the registry file here (TREC-20-19.pdf): same revision 05-04-2026, same 12 pages, with 279 named AcroForm fields. The full field-name inventory is in TREC-20-19-field-names.txt. Map those existing field names to Term keys as the brief describes; no field layer is needed.

Fallback if a future revision's fillable edition is unavailable: build the field map as a coordinate field layer over the untouched flat official pages (widget annotations positioned on each blank, named by Term key). Never recreate or re-typeset the form itself.

The 40-11 and 36-11 are natively fillable; map their existing field names to Term keys as the brief describes.

Revision watch: TREC revises forms on adoption cycles (the 20-19 and 36-11 changed effective 07/01/2026). When TREC posts a new revision, the registry entry is replaced, the field layer/map is re-checked field by field, and previously exported packets are unaffected.
